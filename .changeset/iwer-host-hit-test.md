Host mode hit test: `requestHitTestSource` works in host mode and hit-tests the planes and meshes the host reports through `getPlanes` / `getMeshes` (read for hit test also when the session has no plane or mesh detection), nearest first, with +Y along the surface normal. As in Quest Browser, mesh triangles are hit on their front face only, and a source without `entityTypes` hits the global mesh alone when the room has one, else the planes; a large mesh's bounding-volume tree is built a slice per frame (`XRHostHitTest.buildBudgetMs`), the mesh left out until it is ready. Host-mode frames do not keep the results of cancelled sources; `getHitTestResultsForTransientInput` throws `InvalidStateError` for a cancelled source. Adds `requestHitTestSourceForTransientInput` / `XRFrame.getHitTestResultsForTransientInput` (`XRTransientInputHitTestSource`, `XRTransientInputHitTestResult`), installs `XRRay` and the hit-test interfaces as globals in host mode, and exports `overwriteTransformMatrix` from `iwer/host`. `new XRRay()` now applies the IDL defaults to members left out of its origin and direction dictionaries (`new XRRay({}, { x: 0, y: -1, z: 0 })` no longer throws). The emulator's SEM hit test is unchanged.
